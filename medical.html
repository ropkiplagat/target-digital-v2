Built for medical — with the lines it will never cross

A receptionist for a medical practice can't behave like a chatbot. These aren't features — they're hard rules, baked in and enforced in the code.

  • ✓Emergencies go to 000. If a caller describes anything urgent, it stops and tells them to hang up and call triple zero straight away. It won't try to book or chat.
  • ✓It never gives medical advice. No symptoms, no diagnosis, no medication or results guidance — ever. Clinical questions go to a message for the practice.
  • ✓It only takes what's needed to book. Name, a contact, the appointment type, and a preferred time — no date of birth, no reason-for-visit, no symptoms. Booking details only; no medical data captured.
  • ✓It's honest that it's an AI. If asked, it says so, and offers to take a message for a person.

It's the overflow catcher — not a replacement for your team

Your front desk stays exactly as it is. The AI simply catches the calls that are currently going to voicemail or ringing out. You keep the patients you're already losing — nothing changes about the ones you already answer.
