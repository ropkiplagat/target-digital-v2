The Stack

Five Components. One Calling Machine.
Zero Manual Dialling.

Each piece of the Outbound Call Engine handles one job. Together they catch a lead the instant it arrives, call it, qualify it by voice, and route it — continuously, without you touching the phone.

Form + Webhook

Connects to your landing page, ad form, or website. The moment a lead submits, a webhook fires the engine — no delay, no CSV exports, no manual import.

Twilio
SMS + Telephony

Sends an instant branded SMS to the lead, then places the outbound phone call from a local number — all programmatically, within seconds of the form submit.

AI Voice Agent
Qualify by Voice

A natural-sounding AI agent talks to the lead, asks your qualifying questions, handles objections, and scores them hot, warm, or not-a-fit — in real time, on the call.

Live Transfer + Calendly
Routing

Hot leads are transferred live to your sales line while they're still on the phone. Warm leads are booked straight into your Calendly. Nothing falls through the cracks.

Railway
24/7 Infrastructure

Claude Code agents run the whole flow on Railway — capturing, calling, qualifying, routing, and retrying no-answers continuously. No server to manage. It runs while you sleep.

The Process

From Form Submit to Live Transfer
— in Under a Minute.

01
Lead Submits Form

A prospect fills in your form — landing page, ad, or website. A webhook fires the engine instantly. The clock starts the second they hit submit.

02
Instant SMS

Within seconds, the lead gets a branded text confirming you've received their enquiry and that a call is coming. Expectation set, trust built, before they cool off.

03
AI Call Triggered

The engine places an outbound phone call from a local number — typically in under 60 seconds. No queue, no waiting for a rep to be free. Every lead, every time.

04
AI Qualifies by Voice

A natural-sounding AI agent asks your qualifying questions, handles objections, and scores the lead hot, warm, or not-a-fit — live, on the call.

Then the engine routes every call automatically — three outcomes, zero manual handling:

🔥 HOT
Live Transfer to Sales

Qualified and ready to buy? The AI transfers the lead live to your sales team while they're still on the phone — warm, briefed, and primed to close.

🌤️ WARM
Appointment Booked

Interested but not ready right now? The AI books them straight into your Calendly at a time that suits — and sends the confirmation and reminders automatically.

📵 NO ANSWER
3-Day Retry Logic

Didn't pick up? The engine retries on a smart schedule across the next 3 days — different times of day — plus follow-up SMS, until you connect or the lead is exhausted.
